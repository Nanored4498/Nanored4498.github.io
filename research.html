Quad Mesh Quantization Without a T-Mesh

, David Desobry, Martin Heistermann, David Bommes, Nicolas Ray, Dmitry Sokolov

Eurographics

Quad remeshing via global parametrization is a mixed-integer optimization problem, and common strategies rely on a so-called quantization step. We propose a new quantization algorithm that is simpler than previous ones, as it does not require computing an embedded T-mesh. Instead, it relies on triangular mesh decimation.

PACE Solver Description: Touiouidth

Gaétan Berthe, , Alexander Dobler, Laure Morelle, Amadeus Reinald, Mathis Rocton

International Symposium on Parameterized and Exact Computation (IPEC)

A twin-width solver written in C++. Given a graph, it computes its twin-width along with an associated contraction sequence. It was submitted to the exact track of the 2023 PACE Challenge, where it ranked third 🥉.
