Yoann COUDERT--OSMONT

pro@ycoudert.fr

I am a research engineer in the MIND team at Inria Saclay, maintaining Joblib, a parallel computing and disk caching Python library.
Previously, I received my PhD in Computer Science at Centre Inria de l'Université de Lorraine under the supervision of Dmitry Sokolov and Nicolas Ray. Even earlier, I completed a Master's degree in applied mathematics for machine learning (MVA) from ENS Paris-Saclay and studied fundamental computer science as a civil servant student at ENS de Lyon.

My background is mainly in geometry processing and computer graphics. Beyond that, I'm interested in algorithmics and operations research. I am active in competitive programming. By the time, I specialized in heuristic contests, reaching Google Hash Code world finals and getting a third place at the ICPC Challenge Championship in Dongguan (China).

Outside of work, I love hiking ⛰️, cycling 🚴, and trail running 🏃.
